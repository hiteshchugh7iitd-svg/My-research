# Sakurai Seminar Website — Simple Step-by-Step Guide

Written for people who are not technical. No special skills are needed. If you can use email and upload a photo to Facebook, you can use this website.

**Website address:** https://main.d1emmy6bi3ik7m.amplifyapp.com
**Where staff manage the site:** the same address with `/admin` at the end
**Where students manage their own page:** the same address with `/portal` at the end

> **Golden rule:** after every change, look for a **Save** button and click it. If you do not click Save, nothing changes. A small message at the bottom of the screen ("saved") confirms it worked.

---

## PART 1 — The basics

### Who can do what?

| Person | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Professor** (Prof. Sakurai) | Everything, including choosing who is an Admin. |
| **Admin** | Add and change anything on the website: students, news, photos, papers. Invite students. Approve what students send. |
| **Student** | Change their own page only. Send news or papers to the Admin for approval. |
| **Visitor** | Just reads the website. Cannot change anything. |

Nobody can create an account by themselves. Someone with the right to do so has to **invite** them first.

### How to sign in (first time)

1. Check your email for a message from **Amazon / Cognito** with the subject *"Your account for the Aiko Sakurai Seminar website"*. If you cannot find it, look in **Spam / Junk**.
2. Open the website address above.
3. Click **Sign in** (top right).
4. Type your **email address** and the **temporary password** from the email (copy and paste it, exactly as written).
5. The website asks you to choose a **new password**. It must have:
   - at least 8 characters
   - a capital letter (A–Z) and a small letter (a–z)
   - a number (0–9)
   - a symbol such as `! # ? @`
6. Done. Staff arrive at the **Admin** area. Students arrive at **My page**.

The temporary password stops working after **7 days**. If it expires, ask an Admin to send a new invitation (see "Resend an invitation" below).

### How to sign in (next times)

Open the website → **Sign in** → your email and your own password.

### I forgot my password

Open the website → **Sign in** → click **Forgot your password?** → type your email → check your email for a code → type the code and a new password.

### How to sign out

Click your name or **My page** at the top, then **Sign out**. Always sign out if you used a shared or public computer.

## PART 2 — For Admins and the Professor

Sign in, then click **Admin** in the top menu. A menu appears on the left side. Everything below is found in that left menu.

### 2.1 Add a new student to the website

1. Left menu → **Students & alumni**.
2. Click the green **+ Add student** button (top right).
3. A panel slides in from the right. Fill in:
   - **Name** — required, for example *Hinata TANAKA*
   - **Name in Japanese** — optional
   - **Programme** — choose from the list (Master's, Doctoral, etc.)
   - **Status** — choose **Current**
   - **Year of entry** — for example 2026
4. Scroll down to **Profile** (optional now — the student can fill it in later) and add a photo if you have one.
5. Click **Save** at the bottom.

The student now appears on the public **Students** page.

### 2.2 Invite a student so they can sign in

Do this right after step 2.1, or later by clicking **Edit** next to the student's name.

1. In the panel, find the box **"Give … a login"** at the top.
2. Type the student's **email address**.
3. Click **Send invitation**.
4. The student receives an email with a temporary password (see "How to sign in" above).

When it works, the box turns green and says **"Login linked"**. In the list the student shows a green **Linked** tag.

> Always invite students from their own record like this. It connects their login to their own page, so they can only change their own page and no one else's.

### 2.3 Add many students at once (a list from Excel)

1. Left menu → **Import & export** → tab **Students & alumni**.
2. Click **Download template**. Open it in Excel.
3. Fill in one student per row, then **Save As → CSV**.
4. Back on the website, drag the CSV file into the box.
5. A preview table appears. Check it. Names that already exist show as "will update"; new names show as "new".
6. Click **Import**.

(You still have to send each student their invitation — see 2.2.)

### 2.4 Edit or remove a student

- **Edit:** Students & alumni → click **Edit** next to the name → change what you need → **Save**.
- **Student has finished studying:** click **Graduate** next to the name. They move to the **Alumni** page.
- **Hide without deleting:** open the student, untick **Show on the website**, **Save**.
- **Delete completely:** open the student → **Delete** (bottom left) → confirm. Then also remove their login: left menu → **Accounts & roles** → **Delete** next to their email.

### 2.5 Invite another Admin (Professor only)

1. Left menu → **Accounts & roles**.
2. In the **Invite someone** box type their **email** and **name**.
3. In the third box choose **admin**.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

They sign in the same way as everyone else (Part 1) and see the **Admin** area.

To change a person's role later: in the same page use the **Role** drop-down next to their name.
To stop someone's access for a while: click **Disable** (and **Enable** to bring it back).

> Keep at least **two** people with Professor or Admin rights, so the website never gets locked.

### 2.6 Resend an invitation

If the temporary password expired or the email was lost:
Left menu → **Accounts & roles** → find the person (status "Invited") → click **Resend**.

## PART 3 — Photos

### 3.1 Change a student's photo (Admin)

1. Left menu → **Students & alumni** → **Edit** next to the student.
2. Scroll down to **Profile → Profile photo**.
3. Click the dotted box (or drag a photo from your computer into it).
4. Wait until the new picture appears.
5. Click **Save**.

To take a photo away: click the small **×** on the picture, then **Save**.

### 3.2 Change your own photo (Student)

1. Sign in → **My page** → tab **My profile**.
2. Find **Profile photo** → click the dotted box → choose a photo.
3. Click **Save profile** at the bottom.

Tip: a portrait photo (taller than wide) looks best. Big photos are shrunk automatically.

### 3.3 Change the big photos at the top of the Home page (Admin)

1. Left menu → **Cover page & site text** → tab **Home page**.
2. Find the block **Cover slideshow**. Each row is one slide.
3. Click **Edit** on a slide.
4. Click the dotted box under **Photo** to replace the picture. You can also type a **Headline** and **Text** for that slide.
5. Click **Save** at the top of that block.

To add another slide: click **+ Add** under the list, upload a photo, then **Save**.
To remove a slide: click **Remove** on that row, then **Save**.
To change the order: use the **↑ ↓** arrows, then **Save**.

### 3.4 Add photos to the Gallery (Admin)

**Easiest way — a whole album at once:**
1. Left menu → **Upload center**.
2. Click the dotted box and select **many photos together** (hold Ctrl or Shift to select several).
3. Choose an existing album from the drop-down, or choose **+ New album…** and type a name, such as *Internship AY 2025*.
4. Wait until every photo shows **Uploaded**. Done — no Save button needed here.

**Another way — inside an album:**
1. Left menu → **Gallery** → **+ Add album** (or **Edit** next to an existing one).
2. Type a title (for example *Field study, Ishinomaki*).
3. Under **Photos**, drop in your pictures. You can type a caption under each one.
4. Click **Save**.

"AY 2025" means the school year April 2025 – March 2026.

### 3.5 Add photos to a news post

Open the post (left menu → **News & events** → **Edit**) → under **Photo gallery** drop in the pictures → **Save**.

## PART 4 — News, events and papers

### 4.1 Write a news post or announce an event (Admin)

1. Left menu → **News & events** → **+ Add post**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — required
   - **Category** — News, Event, Student activity, Internship, Fieldwork, Conference…
   - **Publish date** — today by default
   - **Event date** — only for events (it then shows in "Upcoming events")
   - **Summary** — one or two sentences (shown on the small cards)
   - **Full text** — the whole story
   - **Cover photo** — the main picture
3. Make sure **Published** is ticked (untick it to keep a private draft).
4. Click **Save**. It appears on the website straight away.

Writing tips for **Full text**: leave a blank line between paragraphs. Put `##` before a line to make a heading. Put `**` on both sides of a word to make it **bold**.

To change or delete it later: **News & events** → **Edit** → change → **Save**, or **Delete** at the bottom left.

### 4.2 Add a research paper / publication (Admin)

1. Left menu → **Publications** → **+ Add publication**.
2. Fill in: **Type** (Paper, Book, Report or Talk), **Year**, **Title**, **Authors**, **Journal / conference**.
3. If there is a **DOI** (like `10.20965/jdr.2026.p0138`), type it — the website makes a clickable link.
4. To attach the paper itself, use **PDF** and drop the file in. *Only do this if the publisher allows the paper to be shared publicly.*
5. Tick **Peer-reviewed** if it applies. Tick **Feature on home page** to show it on the Home page.
6. Click **Save**.

Many papers at once: **Import & export** → tab **Publications** → same CSV method as in 2.3.

### 4.3 Student sends news or a paper (Student)

Students cannot publish directly. They send it, and an Admin approves it.

**To send news (an internship, fieldwork, conference, award):**
1. Sign in → **My page** → tab **Submit news / report**.
2. Fill in the title, category, date, location, a short summary and the full report. Add photos.
3. Click **Send for approval**.

**To send a paper:** **My page** → **Submit publication** → fill in the details → (PDF optional) → **Send for approval**.

**To follow what happened:** **My page** → **My submissions**. You will see **Waiting**, **Published**, or **Returned** (with a note saying what to fix). A "Waiting" item can be withdrawn.

### 4.4 Approve what students send (Admin)

1. A number appears next to **Submissions** in the left menu when something is waiting.
2. Click **Submissions** → **Review** next to an item.
3. Read it. Then choose:
   - **Approve & publish** — it goes live straight away, or
   - **Return with note** — type a short note (what to change), then click. The student sees your note.

After approving, you can still improve the post under **News & events**.

### 4.5 A student adds papers or slides to their own page (Student)

**My page** → **My profile** → scroll to **Documents** → drop in the file (PDF, Word, PowerPoint) and type a **title** under it → **Save profile**. Visitors can then open it from your page.

You can also upload your **CV** (PDF) in the same screen.

## PART 5 — Student: keep your own page up to date

1. Sign in → **My page** → **My profile**.
2. Fill in whatever you like: photo, CV, research topic (English / 日本語), a short biography, country, thesis title, current position (for alumni), research interests, links (ORCID, LinkedIn…).
3. Click **Save profile**.
4. Click **View my page** to see how visitors see it.

Your name, programme and year of entry are kept by the office. If they are wrong, ask an Admin.

## PART 7 — Safety and good habits

- **Save a backup every month:** left menu → **Import & export** → **Download backup**. Keep the file in a shared folder.
- **Do not share your password.** Every person should have their own login.
- **Sign out** on shared computers.
- **Private information:** the website is public. Do not upload anything confidential (passports, marks, personal phone numbers).
- **Photos of people:** only upload photos if the people in them agree.
- **Videos:** do not upload videos. Put them on YouTube and add the link.
- **Two people editing at once:** if the website says an item "was changed in another tab or by another administrator", click **Cancel** to see their version, or **OK** to replace it with yours.
- **Mistakes:** the left menu **Activity log** shows who changed what and when.

## PART 8 — Problems and quick answers

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I did not get the invitation email | Check Spam / Junk. Then ask an Admin to click **Resend** (Accounts & roles). Check the email address was typed correctly. |
| "Incorrect username or password" | Use **Forgot your password?** If you are new, the temporary password may have expired — ask for **Resend**. |
| A student cannot change their page | Admin: open the student — it must say **Linked**. If not, send the invitation from the student's record (2.2). |
| A photo will not upload | Check the internet, then click **Retry** next to the file. Try a smaller photo. |
| I changed something but the website looks the same | Did you click **Save**? Then refresh the page (press **F5**). |
| I cannot see the **Admin** button | Only Professor and Admin accounts have it. Ask the Professor to check your role (Accounts & roles). |
| The page says **"Something went wrong"** | Press **F5** to reload. If it keeps happening, tell the site maintainer. |
