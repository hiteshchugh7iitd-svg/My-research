I am a Director in the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India, and a member of the Indian Telecom Service (2015 batch), posted in Delhi. Since 2025 I have been studying for a master's degree at the Graduate School of International Cooperation Studies, Kobe University, on the Japan Development Scholarship (JDS), under the supervision of Prof. Aiko Sakurai.

My research asks a simple question: when an authority issues a flood or heat warning, what actually happens between the alert and a school's decision to act? My work combines public-sector experience in communications and alert systems with field research on school disaster resilience.
