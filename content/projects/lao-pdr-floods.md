Lao PDR can detect a flood and decide to close schools, but learning from each disaster is incomplete. This project is a qualitative comparative multiple-case study of four flood-exposed government schools in one district, taking the school-level decision episode as the unit of analysis.

**Main question.** How did principals receive, interpret and use flood-warning information when deciding whether and when to suspend on-site classes during a recent flood?

**What the platform covers.** The national school register (12,479 public institutions, 148 districts), a hazard map, the legal mandate for who may warn and who must act, field evidence, and a draft SOP.
