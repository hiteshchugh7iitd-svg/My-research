This is my main thesis project: a study of the alert-to-action chain for heatwaves in Delhi government schools, built on the SHEW-GF framework (School Hazard Early Warning, Governance Framework) and compared with Japan as a reference case.

Sections of the dashboard cover the heat and school map, the multi-hazard map, the alert chain, the framework, research questions and propositions, and the research design.
