Privacy Policy — FitHitesh

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Summary

FitHitesh keeps your data on your own phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive your data.

What the app stores

Your profile (name, sex, age, height, weights), food diary, water, steps, workouts, sports sessions, sleep, mood and journal notes. All of it is saved only in the app's local storage on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Barcode lookups: when you scan or type a barcode, only that barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to get the product's nutrition. No personal data is sent.
  • Fonts: the Android app ships with its typefaces built in, so nothing is downloaded for them.
  • Backups: if you tap “Export backup” or “Export CSV”, a file is saved to your device. Where you put it after that is your choice.

Camera

The barcode scanner uses Google Play services’ code scanner, which reads the barcode and returns only the number to the app. The app itself has no camera permission, and no images or video are stored or sent anywhere.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android’s own device backup, Android may include the app’s data in your personal Google account backup. That backup belongs to you; the developer cannot see it.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → “Erase all data”, or uninstall the app / clear its storage. Because we hold no copy, deletion is immediate and complete.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Health disclaimer

Calorie, nutrition and activity numbers are estimates and are not medical advice. Consult a doctor before making major changes to your diet or exercise.
